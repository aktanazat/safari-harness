# AAA

Membership is managed at mwg.aaa.com; sign-in runs on auth.mwg.aaa.com. Insurance quotes are a separate site, quote.csaa-insurance.aaa.com, with its own notes.

## Sign-in
- Sign-in is two pages: email then Continue, then password. Run `passwords fill` on each page.

## Membership
- Auto-renew: Account > Payments > Edit Payment Details > Turn Off Automatic Renewal, then confirm "Turn off automatic renewal?".
- Cancel by phone: 888-853-0624, Mon-Fri 9-5:30 PT. A refund is pro-rated only with no roadside use this term and at least 60 days of membership.

## Questions
- /frequently-asked-questions/ask: the first Submit shows suggested articles; Submit again to send. It is sent at "Thank you for reaching out!".
- The words "protected by reCAPTCHA" are on the form, so do not wait for "captcha" as a sign of a bot check.
