# Intel

Intel accounts sign in on consumer.intel.com, a single sign-in page for all of intel.com.

## Sign-in
- Start from the Sign In button on the intel.com home page. /content/www/us/en/my-intel/login.html is a 404.
- An email with no Intel account goes straight to the create-account form. That is the sign there is no account; do not create one.
